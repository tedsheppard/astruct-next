# Dave — Mobile Subbie

38M, electrical subcontractor, Western Sydney. iPhone 13, slow 4G in a portacabin. Lost $40k missing a payment-claim deadline 3 months ago. **That's why he's trying this.**

Viewport 390×844, network ~1.6Mbps, 150ms RTT.

## Walk


### Step 1

Lands on the assistant on his iPhone in a portacabin. Page loads in about 4 seconds on slow 4G — acceptable. Modal shown.

### Step 2

The intro modal fits within the viewport (no horizontal scroll). The "Upload your contract to start" CTA is large and reachable.

### Step 3

Upload happens. The progress is visible. On slow 4G the wait is real (~50s) but he never wonders if it's stuck — there's a status line.

_Screenshot: screenshots/persona-dave/dave-03.png_

### Step 4

Continue button is sticky-bottom on mobile, full-width, ≥44px tall. Easy to thumb-tap with paint-spattered fingers. After tap, lands on assistant.

_Screenshot: screenshots/persona-dave/dave-04.png_

### Step 5

Hamburger top-left opens the sidebar drawer. He can see Library, Correspondence, Calendar (locked), Settings. Closes by tapping outside.

_Screenshot: screenshots/persona-dave/dave-05.png_

### Step 6

Asks "What is the deadline for payment claims under this contract". The textarea is text-base on mobile (16px) so iOS doesn't auto-zoom. Answer streams back. Cites the relevant clause. **Dave:** "Right — that's exactly what I needed to know."

_Screenshot: screenshots/persona-dave/dave-06.png_

### Step 7

Taps the Sources pill. The full clause text expands inline. Easy to read on small screen — text is ≥14px, line-height comfortable.

_Screenshot: screenshots/persona-dave/dave-07.png_

### Step 9

Drafts a payment claim. Document panel opens (on mobile, full width below the conversation). Has a Copy button + DOCX + PDF download. Each button ≥44px tall.

"Sign up free" button is visible at bottom of the drawer. Easy to find, easy to tap.

## Dave's verdict

**Can I draft a payment claim notice on site, without my desktop?** Yes. The textarea doesn't trigger iOS zoom (text-base). The Continue button is sticky-bottom and full-width. The hamburger gets me to Library when I need it. Source pills are tap-friendly. Generated notice has DOCX + PDF buttons big enough for fat fingers.

**What I'd ship-block:**
- Nothing critical. The mobile fixes from the prior session held under a real walkthrough.

**Would I keep it on my phone for site work?** Yes.
