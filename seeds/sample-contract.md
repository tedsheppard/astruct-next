# Head Contract

## Project: Riverside Industrial Estate — Warehouse 12

**Principal:** Riverside Industrial Pty Ltd (ACN 612 345 678)
**Contractor:** Murchison Construction Pty Ltd (ACN 098 765 432)

## Recitals

A. The Principal owns the land at 24–30 Mendelson Drive, Hemmant, QLD ("the Site").
B. The Principal wishes to engage the Contractor to design and construct a new warehouse and associated site works ("the Works") at the Site.
C. The Contractor has agreed to carry out the Works on the terms set out in this Contract.

---

## Clause 1 — Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Contract:

- "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Brisbane, Queensland.
- "Direction" means a direction in writing given by the Superintendent under this Contract, including a Variation, an instruction, an order, a request, an approval or a refusal.
- "Notice" means a notice in writing given in accordance with clause 41.
- "Programme" means the construction programme submitted by the Contractor and accepted (or deemed accepted) under clause 32.
- "Practical Completion" has the meaning given in AS 4000 clause 1.

1.2 Time is of the essence in this Contract.

1.3 Any reference to "days" is to calendar days unless otherwise specified.

---

## Clause 12 — Variations

12.1 The Superintendent may, at any time before Practical Completion, direct the Contractor in writing to perform a Variation, including additions, omissions, substitutions or changes to the character, quality, scope, manner or sequence of the Works.

12.2 Where the Contractor considers that a Direction other than a Variation Direction would constitute a Variation if implemented, the Contractor must, **within 5 Business Days of receiving the Direction**, give the Superintendent a Notice setting out:
   (a) the substance of the Direction;
   (b) the basis on which the Contractor considers it to constitute a Variation; and
   (c) the Contractor's preliminary estimate of the cost and time impact.

If the Contractor does not give the Notice within 5 Business Days, the Contractor is taken to have agreed that the Direction is not a Variation and will have no claim for any cost or time impact arising from it.

12.3 Within 10 Business Days of giving a Notice under clause 12.2, the Contractor must give a further Notice setting out a detailed quotation for the Variation, including:
   (a) the proposed adjustment to the Contract Sum;
   (b) the proposed adjustment to the Date for Practical Completion;
   (c) any consequential effect on the Programme; and
   (d) supporting calculations and documentation.

12.4 The Superintendent must, **within 10 Business Days of receiving the quotation under clause 12.3**, either:
   (a) accept the quotation and issue a Variation Direction;
   (b) reject the quotation, stating reasons; or
   (c) direct the Contractor to proceed pending a determination under clause 12.5.

12.5 If the parties cannot agree on the cost or time impact of a Variation, the Superintendent must determine those impacts within 15 Business Days of the Contractor giving notice of disagreement, acting reasonably and in accordance with the principles in clause 36.

## Clause 18 — Latent Conditions

18.1 If the Contractor encounters a Latent Condition, the Contractor must give the Superintendent a Notice **within 2 Business Days** of becoming aware of the Latent Condition, identifying:
   (a) the location and nature of the Latent Condition;
   (b) the steps the Contractor proposes to take; and
   (c) a preliminary estimate of additional cost and delay.

18.2 If the Contractor fails to give a Notice within the time required by clause 18.1, the Contractor's entitlement to claim additional cost or extension of time arising from the Latent Condition is reduced by the prejudice (if any) suffered by the Principal as a result of the late Notice.

18.3 The Superintendent must determine any claim for additional cost or extension of time arising from a Latent Condition under clause 36, and any determination must include reasons.

---

## Clause 24 — Practical Completion

24.1 The Contractor must achieve Practical Completion of the Works by the Date for Practical Completion.

24.2 When the Contractor considers Practical Completion has been reached, the Contractor must give the Superintendent a Notice. The Superintendent must, within 14 Business Days of receiving the Notice, either:
   (a) issue a Certificate of Practical Completion specifying the date Practical Completion was achieved; or
   (b) give the Contractor a Notice listing the work, services and documents required before Practical Completion can be certified.

24.3 If the Superintendent gives a Notice under clause 24.2(b), the Contractor must complete the listed items and re-issue a Notice under clause 24.2.

---

## Clause 32 — Programme

32.1 The Contractor must submit a Programme to the Superintendent within 10 Business Days of the Date of Acceptance.

32.2 The Superintendent may, within 10 Business Days of receiving the Programme, give the Contractor a Notice approving, conditionally approving, or rejecting the Programme. If the Superintendent does not give a Notice within that period, the Programme is deemed approved.

32.3 The Contractor must update the Programme monthly and submit each update to the Superintendent within 5 Business Days of the end of each month.

---

## Clause 34 — Delay and Extension of Time

34.1 If the Contractor is or will be delayed in reaching Practical Completion by a Qualifying Cause of Delay, the Contractor may claim an extension of time.

34.2 To claim an extension of time, the Contractor must give the Superintendent a Notice **within 10 Business Days of becoming aware** of the Qualifying Cause of Delay, setting out:
   (a) the cause of the delay;
   (b) the date the delay commenced and (if known) the date it ceased;
   (c) the consequences of the delay on the Programme; and
   (d) the extension of time claimed.

34.3 If the Contractor does not give the Notice within the time required by clause 34.2, the Contractor's entitlement to an extension of time arising from that cause is barred. This is an essential time bar and the Principal does not waive it by considering or determining a late claim.

34.4 The Superintendent must determine the extension of time within 15 Business Days of receiving the Notice under clause 34.2, and any determination must be in writing with reasons.

34.5 A Qualifying Cause of Delay includes:
   (a) Variations directed under clause 12;
   (b) Latent Conditions notified under clause 18;
   (c) inclement weather exceeding the allowance in Item 25 of Schedule 1;
   (d) suspension of the Works by the Principal under clause 37;
   (e) industrial action not caused by the Contractor; and
   (f) any other cause expressly stated to be a Qualifying Cause of Delay in this Contract.

---

## Clause 35 — Liquidated Damages

35.1 If the Contractor fails to reach Practical Completion by the Date for Practical Completion as adjusted, the Contractor must pay the Principal liquidated damages of **$8,500 per Business Day** until the earlier of Practical Completion and termination of this Contract.

35.2 Liquidated damages are a genuine pre-estimate of damage and not a penalty.

---

## Clause 36 — Payment

36.1 The Contractor may submit a Payment Claim to the Superintendent on or after the 25th day of each month for the value of the Works carried out up to and including that day.

36.2 Each Payment Claim must:
   (a) identify the construction work to which the claim relates;
   (b) state the amount claimed;
   (c) include a calculation of the value of work done since the last Payment Claim;
   (d) attach supporting evidence reasonably required by the Superintendent; and
   (e) be served in accordance with clause 41.

36.3 The Superintendent must, **within 10 Business Days of receiving a Payment Claim**, give the Contractor a Payment Schedule setting out:
   (a) the amount the Superintendent proposes to certify;
   (b) reasons for any difference from the amount claimed; and
   (c) reasons for withholding payment of any part of the claimed amount.

36.4 The Principal must pay the certified amount within 15 Business Days of the date the Superintendent issues the Payment Schedule.

36.5 If the Principal does not pay a Payment Schedule amount by the due date, the Contractor may charge interest on the unpaid amount at 2% per annum above the RBA cash rate, calculated daily.

---

## Clause 37 — Suspension by the Principal

37.1 The Principal may, by Notice, suspend the Contractor's performance of the Works in whole or in part for any reason.

37.2 If the suspension exceeds 20 Business Days in aggregate, the Contractor may, on 5 Business Days' Notice, terminate this Contract for convenience under clause 39.

37.3 The Contractor is entitled to costs reasonably incurred by reason of the suspension, recoverable through a Variation Direction under clause 12.

---

## Clause 38 — Suspension by the Contractor

38.1 If the Principal fails to pay an amount certified under clause 36.4 within 5 Business Days after the due date, the Contractor may, by Notice giving the Principal 5 Business Days to remedy the default, suspend the Works.

38.2 If the Principal does not pay the outstanding amount within the 5 Business Day cure period, the Contractor may suspend the Works until payment is made and is entitled to recover the cost of suspension as a Variation under clause 12.

---

## Clause 39 — Termination for Convenience

39.1 The Principal may terminate this Contract for convenience at any time by giving the Contractor **20 Business Days' Notice**.

39.2 On termination for convenience, the Contractor is entitled to:
   (a) the value of the work performed to the date of termination;
   (b) reasonable demobilisation and re-mobilisation costs;
   (c) committed but uncancellable supplier and subcontractor costs; and
   (d) a reasonable allowance for loss of profit on the unperformed work.

39.3 The Contractor must mitigate its loss arising from a termination for convenience.

---

## Clause 40 — Defects Liability

40.1 The Defects Liability Period is 12 months from the date of Practical Completion.

40.2 During the Defects Liability Period, the Contractor must rectify any defect notified by the Superintendent within the time stated in the Notice (or, if no time is stated, within a reasonable time).

40.3 If the Contractor fails to rectify a defect within the time required, the Principal may rectify the defect and recover the cost as a debt or set it off against any amount otherwise payable to the Contractor.

40.4 The Defects Liability Period for any rectified defect is extended by 12 months from the date of rectification, in respect of that part of the Works only.

---

## Clause 41 — Notices and Service

41.1 A Notice under this Contract must be in writing and may be given by:
   (a) email to the address listed in Item 30 of Schedule 1;
   (b) hand delivery to the addressee at the address listed in Item 30; or
   (c) registered post to the address listed in Item 30.

41.2 A Notice is taken to have been received:
   (a) if by email — on the next Business Day after sending, unless a delivery failure is received;
   (b) if by hand delivery — on the day of delivery; and
   (c) if by registered post — 3 Business Days after posting.

41.3 Time periods in this Contract run from the day after the day on which the relevant Notice is taken to have been received.

---

## Clause 42 — Dispute Resolution

42.1 If a dispute arises under or in connection with this Contract ("Dispute"), the parties must follow this clause before commencing legal or arbitral proceedings.

42.2 A party claiming a Dispute exists must give the other party a Notice setting out the Dispute and the relief sought ("Dispute Notice"). A Dispute Notice must be given **within 28 days** of the party becoming aware of the matter giving rise to the Dispute.

42.3 Within 10 Business Days of the Dispute Notice, senior representatives of the parties must meet and use reasonable endeavours to resolve the Dispute.

42.4 If the Dispute is not resolved within 20 Business Days of the Dispute Notice, either party may refer the Dispute to mediation administered by the Resolution Institute.

42.5 If the Dispute is not resolved within 20 Business Days of mediation commencing, either party may commence proceedings.

42.6 Nothing in this clause prevents either party from seeking urgent interlocutory relief.

## Special Condition SC.1 — Time Bars

SC.1.1 Compliance with the time bars in clauses 12.2, 18.1, 34.2 and 42.2 is a condition precedent to the Contractor's entitlement to the relief described in those clauses. Failure to comply within the stated period extinguishes the entitlement.

SC.1.2 The Principal does not waive any time bar by considering, responding to, or determining a late claim, except by a Notice expressly waiving the time bar.
