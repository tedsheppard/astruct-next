## Background

This correspondence is issued in the context of groundwater and basalt latent conditions. The immediate issue arises because unexpected groundwater ingress and basalt encounter at b3 excavation, which affects excavation, temporary works, dewatering, programme critical path and requires a clear written record of position, direction, or reservation of rights.


## Contractual Position

The issuing party records that the matter engages Clause 12, Clause 25, Clause 27, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37 of the head contract. The correspondence is intended to preserve the documentary chain between site events, programme impacts, directions, valuation positions, and any later claim or certification outcome.


## Required Action

The receiving party is requested to acknowledge the correspondence, confirm any immediate actions, and respond on the substance of the issue within the timeframe stated in the body of the project letter. Where the matter has programme effect, the receiving party is expected to identify mitigation measures and state whether the event is alleged to affect the critical path.
