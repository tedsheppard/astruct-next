## Formal Instrument of Agreement

This Formal Instrument of Agreement records that Riverside Development Group Pty Ltd engages Apex Construction Pty Ltd to carry out and complete the works known as Riverside Towers - Mixed Use Development at 18 Skyring Terrace, Newstead QLD 4006. The Contractor accepts the engagement and agrees to perform the works for the Contract Sum of $47,850,000.00 subject to adjustments permitted by the contract.


The parties acknowledge that the contract documents comprise this FIA, the annexures, the special conditions, the general conditions, the project requirements, the schedules, the accepted tender return, the clarification register, and every drawing and specification listed in the schedule of contract documents as revised from time to time in accordance with the contract.


## Recitals

The Principal is procuring a Mixed-use residential tower with retail podium and three-level basement comprising 17 storeys above ground, 3 basement levels, 132 apartments, and 4 retail tenancies with a total gross floor area of approximately 22150 square metres.


The Contractor entered the tender process on the basis of the issued project documents, undertook its own investigations, submitted qualifications that were subsequently addressed in post-tender negotiation, and accepted that the final contract would be administered as a design-complete traditional head contract with tightly controlled superintendent directions and rigorous reporting obligations.

## Tender Return Incorporated into Contract

The accepted tender return incorporates the contractor methodology, programme assumptions, resource plan, qualifications resolved during negotiation, priced trade package breakdown, key personnel commitments, and the clarified procurement strategy for long-lead façade, lift, and authority-interface elements.


The post-tender clarifications record that the Contractor was required to remove any assumption inconsistent with A2 façade compliance, to commit to a monthly reporting format acceptable to the Superintendent, and to recognise that document control and design issue status would be central to both valuation and EOT assessments.


## Risk and Event Summary

### EV-01 - Tender clarification and award

Event driver: Tender-stage clarifications, façade combustibility qualifications, and post-tender negotiation. Affected areas include façade performance, programme assumptions, scope interfaces, early procurement. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 13, Clause 14, Clause 17, Clause 25, Clause 37.



### EV-02 - Additional piling

Event driver: Revised geotechnical interpretation requiring test piles and additional core piling. Affected areas include piling, foundation design, excavation sequencing. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 12, Clause 13, Clause 32, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.



### EV-03 - Basement coordination issues

Event driver: Coordination discrepancies between architectural, hydraulic, civil, and structural documents. Affected areas include stormwater detention, services penetrations, loading dock, fire services. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 8, Clause 13, Clause 27, Clause 32, Clause 37.



### EV-04 - January inclement weather

Event driver: Abnormal January storm activity affecting safe access and excavation productivity. Affected areas include excavation, basement slabs, haul roads. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.



### EV-05 - Laneway B access restriction

Event driver: Restricted site access and revised traffic-management controls imposed by adjacent owner activity. Affected areas include traffic control, deliveries, public interface, concrete pours. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 13, Clause 15, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.



### EV-06 - Groundwater and basalt latent conditions

Event driver: Unexpected groundwater ingress and basalt encounter at B3 excavation. Affected areas include excavation, temporary works, dewatering, programme critical path. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 12, Clause 25, Clause 27, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.



### EV-07 - Façade specification change

Event driver: Directed change from original FR panel build-up to A2-compliant façade system. Affected areas include façade panels, support rails, shop drawings, lead times. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 13, Clause 17, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.



### EV-08 - Basement waterproofing upgrade

Event driver: Directed upgrade to composite waterproofing and perimeter drainage following groundwater findings. Affected areas include basement walls, membranes, drainage cells, joint treatment. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 13, Clause 16, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.



### EV-09 - Electrical authority and riser clashes

Event driver: Substation approval hold points and unresolved electrical riser conflicts with transfer structure. Affected areas include substation, electrical risers, beam penetrations, authority submissions. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 13, Clause 17, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.



### EV-10 - Lift procurement and acoustic redesign

Event driver: Extended lift manufacturing lead times and principal-requested acoustic upgrades at retail interface. Affected areas include lifts, acoustic separation, fitout sequencing, commissioning. Claims and assessments arising out of this event normally engage Clause 13, Clause 17, Clause 25, Clause 33, Clause 34, Clause 37.
