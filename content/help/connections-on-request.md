Every connection is read only: Astruct reads what is there and files it to the right project, and never posts, sends or deletes anything in the app. Open **Apps** in Astruct to connect. Each app's row has a **Guide** link to the same guide as below.

## Connect yourself, in one click

- **Outlook and Microsoft 365**, including shared mailboxes. See [Connecting Outlook and Microsoft 365](/support/connecting-outlook).
- **OneDrive**. See [Connecting OneDrive](/support/connecting-onedrive).
- **SharePoint**, once your Microsoft 365 administrator has approved Astruct. See [Connecting SharePoint](/support/connecting-sharepoint) and [Admin consent for Microsoft 365](/support/microsoft-365-admin-consent).
- **Gmail**, **Google Drive** and **Google Calendar**, with your Google account. See [Connecting Gmail](/support/connecting-gmail), [Connecting Google Drive](/support/connecting-google-drive) and [Connecting Google Calendar](/support/connecting-google-calendar).
- **Dropbox**. See [Connecting Dropbox](/support/connecting-dropbox).
- **Autodesk Construction Cloud** (now part of Autodesk Forma) and BIM 360. See [Connecting Autodesk Construction Cloud](/support/connecting-autodesk).

## Connect with keys from your admin

- **Procore**: your Procore company admin makes a custom app for Astruct once, and you enter its keys on Apps. One-click Connect arrives once Procore approves Astruct's Marketplace listing. See [Connecting Procore](/support/connecting-procore).
- **Oracle Aconex**: your Lobby admin makes an OAuth client for Astruct once, and you enter its keys on Apps. See [Connecting Oracle Aconex](/support/connecting-aconex).

## Coming soon

- **Coming soon:** InEight Document (TeamBinder), Box, Xero, MYOB, Payapps and HammerTech. Astruct cannot connect to them yet. See [Connecting InEight Document (TeamBinder)](/support/connecting-ineight), [Connecting Box](/support/connecting-box), [Connecting Xero](/support/connecting-xero), [Connecting MYOB](/support/connecting-myob), [Connecting Payapps](/support/connecting-payapps) and [Connecting HammerTech](/support/connecting-hammertech).

## Everything else

Upload it: files, whole folders, zips and saved emails (.eml, .msg). See [Uploads](/support/connecting-uploads) and [Bringing in documents](/support/bringing-in-documents).

To ask about another system, email info@astruct.io from your work address.
