**Outlook**, **OneDrive** and **SharePoint** connect from the Apps page with your Microsoft 365 sign-in. See [Connecting Outlook](/support/connecting-outlook).

**Gmail**, **Google Drive**, **Dropbox** and **Autodesk Construction Cloud** are live: choose Connect, sign in and approve.

**Procore**, **Aconex**, **InEight / TeamBinder** and **Xero** connect on request. We set each one up with you, so it reads the right projects and folders from day one.

## Asking for one

Tell us when you [request your free trial](/request-trial), or email info@astruct.io from your work address. Say which system you use.

## Until then

Upload what you need from those systems as files, whole folders or zips. See [Bringing in documents](/support/bringing-in-documents).
