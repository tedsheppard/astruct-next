**Outlook** is live for everyone. See [Connecting Outlook](/support/connecting-outlook).

**Procore**, **Gmail** and **SharePoint** are available on request. We set each one up with you, so it reads the right projects, mailboxes and folders from day one.

## Asking for one

Tell us when you [request your free trial](/request-trial), or email info@astruct.io from your work address. Say which system you use and, for Gmail, which Google accounts you want to connect.

## Until then

Upload what you need from those systems as files, whole folders or zips. See [Bringing in documents](/support/bringing-in-documents).
