When Astruct finds a direction that may be a variation, it opens an issue. From the issue, Astruct can draft the variation notice for you to review.

## What goes in

Astruct fills in the particulars from the project:

- the direction, with its date and reference (for example a site instruction, RFI response or revised drawing);
- the clause the notice is given under, from your contract, and any special condition that replaces it;
- why your company considers the direction a variation, set against the scope of work;
- any later step the contract describes, such as when particulars or a quotation will follow;
- the recipients and addresses the contract names for notices.

## Preparing it

1. Open the issue from the project page or from **Needs attention**.
2. Check the evidence and the clause.
3. Choose to prepare the notice. It opens in **Draft**.

You can also ask in a chat: *Draft a variation notice for SI-014.*

## Before you send

- Read it against the clause. The clause text is shown on the issue.
- Check the period against your contract. See [Notice periods and “check the clause”](/support/notice-periods-and-check-the-clause).
- Open it in Word or download the PDF, sign it and send it the way your contract describes.
- Back on the issue, choose **Mark as sent**, and file the sent letter to the project.

If new evidence arrives after a draft is prepared, the issue says the draft needs another look.
