When the agent flags a direction as a possible variation, it opens an issue. From the issue, Astruct can draft the variation notice for you to review.

## What goes in

Astruct fills in the particulars from the project:

- the direction, with its date and reference (for example a site instruction, RFI response or revised drawing);
- the clause the notice is given under, from your contract, and any special condition that replaces it;
- why the direction is considered a variation, based on the scope of work;
- any later step the contract requires, such as when particulars or a quotation will follow;
- the right recipients, where the contract says where notices must be sent.

## Preparing it

1. Open the issue from the project page or from **Needs attention**.
2. Check the evidence and the clause.
3. Choose to prepare the notice. It opens in **Draft**.

You can also ask in a chat: *Draft a variation notice for SI-014.*

## Before you send

- Read it against the clause. The clause text is shown on the issue.
- Check the deadline is right for your contract. See [Deadlines and “check the clause”](/support/deadlines-and-check-the-clause).
- Download the .docx, sign it and send it the way your contract requires.
- Back on the issue, choose **Mark as sent** so the register stays accurate.

If new evidence arrives after a draft is prepared, the issue says the draft needs another look.
