Astruct can read Gmail and Google Workspace mailboxes the same way it reads Outlook: new mail is checked about every five minutes and filed to the right project.

## Why it is a pilot

Google reviews every app that reads Gmail before it can be used by anyone. Until that review is complete, Google only lets a limited list of accounts connect. While that is the case, Gmail is open to pilot customers only.

## Joining the pilot

Email info@astruct.io from your work address and tell us which Google accounts you want to connect. We’ll let you know when they are added.

## Connecting once you’re added

1. Open **Settings > Connections**.
2. Next to **Gmail**, choose **Connect Gmail** and sign in with Google.
3. Google warns that the app hasn’t been verified yet. This is expected during the pilot. Choose to continue and review the permissions.

Astruct never sends mail from your account by itself. See [Nothing is sent automatically](/support/nothing-is-sent-automatically).
