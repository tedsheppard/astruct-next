Everyone in your organisation shares its projects, according to their role. Open **Team** in the sidebar to see who is in it. **Settings > Members** shows the same list.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and who owns the organisation. |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove people, change roles, set mailbox access, manage connections for the organisation and manage billing. |
| **Member** | Work on shared projects: ask, search, draft, upload and manage issues. |
| **Viewer** | Read shared projects without changing them. |

## Invite someone

1. In **Team**, enter their work email.
2. Choose a role.
3. Choose **Invite**.

They get an email invitation. Until they accept, the invitation is listed under the members, where you can send it again or withdraw it.

## Change a role or remove someone

Owners and admins can change a person’s role from the list, or remove them. A person who is removed loses access to the organisation’s projects straight away.

## Users

There is no charge per person. Astruct is priced per project, and everyone in your organisation is included. See [Projects, users and pricing](/support/projects-and-users).
