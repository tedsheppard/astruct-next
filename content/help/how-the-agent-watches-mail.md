You don’t need to ask Astruct to look. Once a mailbox or folder is connected, the agent reads what arrives and tells you when something needs attention.

## What it reads

- Mail in the mailboxes you connect, including attachments. New mail is picked up within about five minutes, sooner for Outlook when Microsoft notifies Astruct of a change.
- Documents in connected SharePoint and OneDrive folders, and documents you upload.

It only sees what you connect. Mail that goes to a mailbox you haven’t connected, or a letter that only arrives on paper, isn’t seen until you add it.

## What it looks for

- **Directions** that may be variations: site instructions, RFI responses, revised drawings, shop drawing comments and emails telling you to do something differently. See [Directions compared against scope](/support/directions-compared-against-scope).
- **Potential delays**: plant breakdowns, late access, late information, revised handover dates and programme changes. See [Delays](/support/delays).
- **Notices and claims** from the other party, and payment claims and schedules, where they start a clock.

## What it does when it finds one

1. Opens an **issue** on the project, or adds to an existing one when it is the same matter.
2. Links the **evidence**, quoting the words from the email or document, each one opening its source.
3. Finds the **clause** that governs it and works out the **deadline**. See [Deadlines and “check the clause”](/support/deadlines-and-check-the-clause).
4. Shows it in **Needs attention** on the project page, sorted by deadline.

You can prepare the notice from the issue. Nothing is sent automatically.

## What it ignores

Mail that uses commercial words without commercial effect, such as a *variation* to a meeting time or an *extension* for a safety document, isn’t raised as an issue.
