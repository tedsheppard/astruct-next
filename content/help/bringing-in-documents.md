A project can hold **up to 10,000 documents**. You can bring them in three ways, and mix them freely.

## Upload

Open the project and go to **Documents**. Drag files or whole folders onto it, or choose **Upload**.

- **Folders** keep their structure, so a drawing register or a correspondence archive arrives the way your team filed it.
- **Zips** are unpacked into their documents.
- **Emails** saved as .eml or .msg are read with their attachments.
- **PDFs**, including scanned PDFs, Word documents and spreadsheets are all read.

Astruct files each document into the project’s folders: Contract, Correspondence, Variations, Delays and EOTs, Notices, Claims, Programme, Site records, Drawings and Other.

## Connect a folder

Connect a SharePoint or OneDrive folder and Astruct keeps it in sync, so new revisions are picked up without anyone uploading them. See [SharePoint and OneDrive](/support/sharepoint-and-onedrive).

## Connect a mailbox

Connect Outlook and attachments from project mail are filed as they arrive. See [Connecting Outlook](/support/connecting-outlook).

## How long it takes

Small uploads are ready in moments. A large archive takes longer, because each page is read so it can be searched and cited. You can keep working while it finishes. Everything you’ve uploaded appears in **Library**, which lists documents across all your projects.

## Tips

- Upload the executed contract, not a tender draft.
- Include the programme and the drawing register. They help Astruct spot delays and revised drawings.
- If a scanned page is hard to read on screen, it is hard for Astruct too. Rescan it if you can.
