Astruct never sends a letter, notice, claim or email, and nothing goes out from your mailbox.

Everything it drafts is marked **Draft for review**. A person on your team reads it, makes any changes, and sends it through your normal process, whether that is email from Outlook, a document control system or a signed letter.

## Why

Notices have legal effect. The person giving one needs to be satisfied it is right, that it goes to the right addresses, and that it is given the way the contract requires.

## After you send

When you have sent a notice, open its issue and choose **Mark as sent**. File the sent letter or email to the project so it appears in the Library and the Chronology.

## Connected mailboxes

Connecting Outlook lets Astruct read mail. Microsoft lists a permission to send mail on the consent screen. Astruct doesn’t send anything: you send from Outlook or your normal process.
