Astruct is priced per project per month, with unlimited users, plus credits for AI use. The AI work itself is paid for in one of two ways. You choose in **Settings > Model account**, and you can change at any time.

## Use Astruct credits

The default. Astruct’s own models, under OpenAI’s business API terms, with no training on your data. Use is metered as credits, shown in **Settings > Billing and credits**. Credits are charged as used, AU$1.00 each, billed monthly.

## Use your own API key

Add your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key. Astruct’s chat then runs on your key, and that provider bills you for it. Those requests go to that provider under your agreement with them.

## Codex and Claude Code on your computer

Your Astruct Computer has a Terminal. You can sign in to your own Codex or Claude Code there and use them yourself, under OpenAI’s or Anthropic’s terms for your account. Astruct’s chat does not use them.
