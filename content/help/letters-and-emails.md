Astruct drafts project correspondence from what it knows about the project: the contract, the documents and the mail.

## Start a draft

- **From a chat:** ask for it. *Draft a letter to Harrow & Vale asking for the TC1 service records.* or *Draft a reply to Rebecca’s email of 28 September about the L6 handover.*
- **From Draft:** open **Draft** in the sidebar and start a new draft for a project.
- **From an issue:** open the issue and choose to prepare the notice. See [Variation notices](/support/variation-notices).

## Edit it

Drafts open in **Draft**, where you can edit the text directly. Your changes save as you type. You can also ask Astruct to change it: *make it firmer*, *add the drawing references*, *shorten the second paragraph*.

## Take it away

Choose **Download .docx** to get a Word file on your letterhead. See [Your letterhead and Word files](/support/letterhead-and-word-files).

Astruct doesn’t send letters or emails for you. See [Nothing is sent automatically](/support/nothing-is-sent-automatically).
