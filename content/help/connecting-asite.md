Asite cannot connect yet. Asite's API signs in only with a person's Asite email address and password, and Astruct never asks for a password. Asite connects once Asite issues an integration credential that is not a person's password.

- **Who does it:** You, by uploading. Your Asite organisation administrator, if you request a connection.
- **How long:** As long as the download and upload take.

## What Astruct reads

- Nothing from Asite directly yet. Astruct reads what you upload: documents, correspondence and transmittals as files.

## Bring Asite documents in now

1. In Asite, download the documents you need, or a zip of them.
2. In Astruct, open the project's Library and select Upload or Folder, or drop the files or the zip onto it. Zips are opened and keep their folders.
3. Forward the Asite mail you need to a mailbox Astruct reads, or print it to PDF and upload it, and Astruct files it as correspondence.

## Ask for a connection

1. On the Asite row in Apps, select Request connection. Enter the organisation name in Asite, your Asite administrator's email and the workspaces.
2. Astruct takes it up with Asite. The row says Requested until then.

## When it is working

- Uploaded files show in the project's Library, each filed by what it is.

## If something goes wrong

- **“Needs more from you”** Astruct has asked a question about your request. The line under it, and an email to you, say what is needed. Select Add details on the row to answer.
