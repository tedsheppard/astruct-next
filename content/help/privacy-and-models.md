## Which models

Astruct uses OpenAI’s API. Your content is sent to the model only to answer a request, and only the parts needed for it.

## Zero Data Retention

Astruct’s OpenAI account has **Zero Data Retention**. OpenAI doesn’t keep your content once a request is answered.

## No training on your data

Your mail, documents and drafts are **never used to train models**, by us or by OpenAI.

## Nothing is sent without a person

Astruct drafts letters and notices for review. It never sends them. See [Nothing is sent automatically](/support/nothing-is-sent-automatically).

## Your privacy rights

How we handle personal information is set out in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy). To ask about your data, email info@astruct.io.
