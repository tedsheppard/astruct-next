## Which models

Astruct uses OpenAI’s API. Your content is sent to the model only to answer a request, and only the parts needed for it.

## Where requests are processed

Model requests are processed by **OpenAI in the United States**, under its business API terms. Your projects, mail, documents and drafts are stored in Australia, in Sydney. See [Where your data is stored](/support/security-overview).

## No training on your data

Under those terms, your mail, documents and drafts are **not used to train models**, by us or by OpenAI.

## If you add your own API key

You can add your own OpenAI API key instead of using Astruct credits ([how](/support/credits-or-your-own-api-key)). Astruct’s chat then sends those requests to that provider under your agreement with them, and the provider bills you. The business API terms and no training above cover work on Astruct credits.

## Astruct never sends

Astruct drafts letters and notices for review. It never sends them, and nothing goes out from your mailbox. You check each draft and send it yourself. See [Nothing is sent automatically](/support/nothing-is-sent-automatically).

## Your privacy rights

How we handle personal information is set out in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy). To ask about your data, email info@astruct.io.
