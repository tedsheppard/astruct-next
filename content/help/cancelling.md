You can cancel any time. Owners and admins manage the subscription.

## How to cancel

1. Open **Settings > Billing and credits**.
2. Choose to manage or cancel your subscription.
3. Confirm.

## During the trial

Cancel before your trial ends and your card is **not charged**. We’ll email you 5 days before the trial ends as a reminder.

## After the trial

Your subscription stops renewing. Any pay-as-you-go credits you have already used are billed.

## Your data

Cancelling doesn’t delete your projects straight away. If you want your data deleted, or want a copy first, email info@astruct.io.

If something isn’t working for you, we’d like to hear why at info@astruct.io.
