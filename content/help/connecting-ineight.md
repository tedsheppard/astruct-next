Coming soon. Astruct cannot connect to InEight Document (TeamBinder) yet.

- **Who does it:** You.
- **How long:** As long as the export and upload take.

## What Astruct reads

- Nothing from InEight Document directly yet. Astruct reads what you upload from it: documents, mail and transmittals as files.

## Meanwhile

1. In InEight Document, download the documents and transmittals you need, or a zip of them.
2. In Astruct, open the project's Library and select Upload or Folder, or drop the files or the zip onto it. Zips are opened and keep their folders.
3. Forward the project mail you need to a mailbox Astruct reads, or print it to PDF and upload it, and Astruct files it as correspondence.

## When it is working

- Uploaded files show in the project's Library, each filed by what it is.
