Coming soon. Astruct cannot connect to Payapps yet.

- **Who does it:** You.
- **How long:** As long as the export and upload take.

## What Astruct reads

- Nothing from Payapps directly yet. Upload the claim and assessment PDFs to the project and Astruct reads them.

## Meanwhile

1. Download the claims and assessments you need from Payapps as PDFs.
2. In Astruct, open the project's Library and select Upload, or drop the files onto it.

## When it is working

- Uploaded claims show in the project's Library, filed as payment claims.

Astruct never asks for your password. Still stuck? Email info@astruct.io.
