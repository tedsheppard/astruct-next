If your project documents live in SharePoint or OneDrive, connect the folder once and Astruct keeps it in sync. New files and new revisions are read without anyone uploading them.

## OneDrive

1. Open **Settings > Connections**.
2. Next to **OneDrive**, choose **Connect OneDrive** and sign in.
3. Choose the folders to keep in sync.

## SharePoint

SharePoint needs your Microsoft 365 administrator to approve Astruct for your organisation once. Send them the approval link from **Settings > Connections**. See [Admin consent for Microsoft 365](/support/microsoft-365-admin-consent).

Once approved:

1. Next to **SharePoint**, choose **Connect SharePoint** and sign in.
2. Pick the site and document library, then the folders for each project.

Astruct only reads the sites and libraries you can open yourself.

## What gets read

PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets and emails saved in the folder, the same as an upload. Each project can hold up to 10,000 documents. See [Bringing in documents](/support/bringing-in-documents).

## Keeping in sync

Astruct checks connected folders regularly and picks up what changed. The project page shows each source and when it last synced, for example “synced 3 min ago”.
