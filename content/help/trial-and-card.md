Your free trial lasts **30 days** and includes **100 credits**.

## Your card

You add a card when you sign up. **It is not charged during the trial.** We ask for it up front so there is no gap in service if you decide to keep going.

## The reminder

**We’ll email you 5 days before your trial ends**, to the address you signed up with. The email says when the trial ends and what happens next.

## When the trial ends

- If you do nothing, your subscription starts: AU$89.95 per user per month, ex GST, with 100 credits per user included each month.
- If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged. See [Cancelling](/support/cancelling).

## If you use all 100 trial credits early

Astruct tells you before a request would go over. You can wait for the trial to end, or start your subscription early from **Settings > Billing and credits**.

You can see how many trial credits you have left, and the date the trial ends, in **Settings > Billing and credits**.
