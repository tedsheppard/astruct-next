You don’t need to know the exact words a document used. In **Search**, choose **Plain English** and describe what you are looking for.

## Examples

- *where the head contractor said the bracket change costs nothing*
- *emails about the crane being out of service*
- *the latest programme’s date for the L6 slab edge handover*
- *any direction to work out of sequence*

Plain-English search finds documents by meaning, so *the crane being out of service* also finds an email that says *TC1 has been out of service since yesterday morning*.

## When to use which

| Use Plain English when | Use Boolean when |
|---|---|
| You know what happened but not the words used | You know the exact words, references or phrases |
| You want the most relevant few results | You need every document that matches |
| You are exploring | You are checking something thoroughly, such as for a claim |

Both search the same documents and mail, and both show the page each result is on. For the Boolean operators, see [Boolean search syntax](/support/boolean-search).

If you want an answer rather than a list of documents, ask the question in a chat instead. See [Asking good questions](/support/asking-good-questions).
