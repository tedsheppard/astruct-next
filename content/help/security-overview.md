## In Australia

Your projects, mail, documents and drafts are stored in Australia, in Sydney. Model requests are processed by OpenAI in the United States. See [Privacy and AI models](/support/privacy-and-models).

## Who can see what

- People see the projects of their organisation, according to their role. See [Members and roles](/support/members-and-roles).
- Mailboxes are private to the person who connected them until an admin gives access. See [Mailbox access](/support/mailbox-access).

## Connections you control

- Each person connects their own mailbox and can disconnect it in **Settings > Connections**.
- Organisation-wide Microsoft 365 access runs on your administrator’s consent, and they can revoke it at any time. See [Admin consent for Microsoft 365](/support/microsoft-365-admin-consent).

## Your account

Turn on two-step verification in **Settings > Security** with an authenticator app. You can change your password there too.

More detail is on the [security page](/security). For a security questionnaire, email info@astruct.io.
