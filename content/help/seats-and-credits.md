## Seats

Astruct is **AU$89.95 per user per month, ex GST**. Each person in your organisation is a seat.

## Credits

Credits measure the AI work Astruct does for you:

- answering questions and running searches that need AI;
- the agent reading new mail and documents, and checking them for directions and delays;
- drafting letters, notices and claims.

Each seat includes **100 credits a month**. Credits are **pooled across your organisation**, so a team of five has 500 credits a month to share, however the work falls between them.

## Pay as you go

If your organisation uses more than its included credits, Astruct keeps working. Extra credits are **AU$1.00 each**, billed monthly with your subscription.

## Seeing your usage

**Settings > Billing and credits** shows your plan, credits used and left this month, your seats and any pay-as-you-go usage.

All prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST.
