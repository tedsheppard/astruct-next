The demo project, *Kestrel Street Mixed-Use, Stage 2*, is a fictional façade subcontract in Brisbane. Every person, company and email address in it is made up.

It comes with a mailbox labelled **Demo mailbox (synthetic)**. The demo mailbox replays the project’s emails through the same pipeline your own mail goes through, so you can watch Astruct:

- file each email to the project and add it to the Chronology;
- open an issue for a site instruction that changes the bracket system, even though it says there is no change to the subcontract sum;
- open an issue for a crane outage and a revised slab edge handover;
- find the notice clauses, show the period each one states and mark them “check the clause”;
- draft the variation notice and the notice of delay for review.

## Things to try

- Ask: *What does the subcontract say about a variation notice for SI-014?*
- Search: `bracket* w/5 ("slab edge" OR anchor*) AND "Rev C"`
- Open the variation issue and look at its evidence and clause.

## Good to know

- The demo mailbox never sends mail and never reads your mailbox.
- Nothing in the demo is real advice about a real contract.
- To ask for the demo project on your account, email info@astruct.io.
