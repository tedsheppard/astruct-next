Every draft downloads as a Word file, so it fits the way your team already signs and sends letters.

## Download

In **Draft** or on an issue, choose **Download .docx**.

## Your house style

Astruct takes your house style from a letter your company has actually sent. Give it a recent letter as a Word file, and it reads:

- the page size and margins;
- the fonts and spacing;
- the letterhead on the first page, including your logo;
- the running header and the footer, with its page numbers.

Every later .docx is built in that style. Astruct copies what the file itself contains, rather than guessing, so the result looks like your own letters.

To set it up, upload the letter to the project and ask Astruct to use it as your house style.

## Editing in Word

Open the file in Word and change what you like. If you want Astruct to make a change instead, ask in the chat or edit in **Draft** and download again.
