Connecting your mailbox lets Astruct read the project mail for variations, delays and claims as it arrives. Each person connects their own mailbox.

## Connect your mailbox

1. Open **Settings > Connections**.
2. Next to **Outlook**, choose **Connect Outlook**.
3. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 account and review the permissions Microsoft shows you.
4. You come back to Astruct, and it starts reading.

The first read goes back through recent mail. After that, **new mail is picked up within about five minutes**, and sooner when Microsoft notifies Astruct of a change. It is not instant.

## Which project does mail go to?

Astruct files each message to the project it belongs to, using the people, references and subjects it recognises. Everything Astruct has read, filed or not, is in **Mail** in the sidebar.

## More than one mailbox

You can connect several mailboxes, up to ten, in any mix of Outlook and Gmail: for example your own work mailbox and a project mailbox.

1. Open **Settings > Connections**.
2. Under **Add another mailbox**, choose **Connect Outlook** or **Connect Gmail** and sign in with the other account.

Each mailbox is read on its own and listed with its address, when it was last checked and whether it needs attention. An email that arrives in two of your mailboxes is filed once. In **Mail**, choose a mailbox to see only its mail; each message shows which mailbox it is in.

When Astruct prepares an email, it goes into the Drafts of the mailbox the original came from. You can choose another of your mailboxes before you send it.

## Shared mailboxes

A shared mailbox you can already open in Outlook, such as a project inbox, can be added without signing in again: in **Settings > Connections**, choose **Shared mailbox** and enter its address. Astruct reads it through your own Outlook connection. Drafts for its mail go into your own Drafts.

If Astruct says your account cannot open it, ask your Microsoft 365 administrator for Full Access to the mailbox. Some organisations also need the administrator to approve Astruct first. See [Admin consent for Microsoft 365](/support/microsoft-365-admin-consent).

## If it says “Needs attention”

Microsoft sometimes asks for permission again, for example after a password change or a policy change. Choose **Reconnect** in **Settings > Connections**. Mail that arrived in the meantime is picked up once you reconnect.

## Disconnecting

Choose **Disconnect** next to the mailbox in **Settings > Connections**. Astruct stops reading that mailbox; your other mailboxes are not affected. Mail and documents already filed stay where they are.

Astruct never sends mail from your mailbox by itself. See [Nothing is sent automatically](/support/nothing-is-sent-automatically).
